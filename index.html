About Me

I found my way into training the hard way. I was the kid picked last, so sport never felt like a home for me. Decades later, when injuries and age began to speak in aches, I had to rebuild from nothing.

Without the solid athletic base that many pros inherit, I had to learn deliberately, patiently, and with respect for every limitation. That beginner's mind stayed with me. Instead of pushing generic athleticism, I focus on clear, purposeful tools that serve your particular goals and honor the body you live in.

Let us worship the quiet gods of strength together—as old warriors once did—yet approach the barbell as a philosophy of movement. We train to grow, to heal, to become resilient; not to grind ourselves into hidden weakness.

“I must study politics and war, that our sons may have liberty to study mathematics and philosophy … in order to give their children a right to study painting, poetry, music.”
